Three changes you will see in the output rather than in your config, and all of them are corrections rather than features. A split page stops sharing one title with all of its parts, a `noindex` you set in the `seo` namespace stops being ignored, and heading anchors stop turning `page.translations` into `pagetranslations`.

Nothing here needs a change on your end. All three will move URLs or markup on the next build, which is why each one says what to look for.

---

## Breaking

{% stepsBlock 'compact' %}

- **Heading anchors changed for headings containing punctuation or symbols.**

  A heading id is now slugified for reading rather than for filing. Dots, slashes and underscores become word boundaries instead of disappearing, and symbols are dropped rather than spelled out as words.

  {% tableBlock true %}

  | Heading | Was | Now |
  | ------- | --- | --- |
  | `page.translations` | `pagetranslations` | `page-translations` |
  | `Add a schema.js file` | `add-a-schemajs-file` | `add-a-schema-js-file` |
  | `The <head> placeholder` | `the-lessheadgreater-placeholder` | `the-head-placeholder` |

  {% endtableBlock %}

  Ordinary prose headings are unaffected, and so are accents and camelCase: `Le fossé pédagogique` still gives `le-fosse-pedagogique`, and `inlineESbuild` still gives `inlineesbuild`.

  On the site this was found on, 23 of 608 headings changed, or 3.8%. Internal links and tables of contents follow automatically, because they read the same id. **What does not follow is a link somebody else wrote to one of your anchors**, or one you wrote by hand in a template.

  - _Symptom: a deep link from outside that used to land on a heading now lands at the top of the page. Search your content for hand-written `#fragment` links if you have headings that name an API._

- **A split page's parts no longer share the first part's title.**

  A page split with `<!--pagebreak-->` now qualifies each part's `<title>` and `og:title` with the part label, which leads so that truncation eats the tail rather than the label.

  Previously every part carried page one's title verbatim. On the site this was found on, that was eighteen URLs with duplicate titles.

  - _Symptom: titles change in Search Console for any split page. This is the fix, not a regression, but it is the kind of thing that shows up there before it shows up in a changelog._

{% endstepsBlock %}

## Fixed

- **`seo.noindex` is read.** It was accepted by the page schema and looked at by nothing, so a page that set it was silently indexed anyway. Three readers honour it now: the robots meta tag, canonical suppression, and sitemap inclusion.

  It resolves as `seo.noindex ?? noindex`, which also means a page can opt back **in** against a `noindex: true` it inherited from a directory data file.

  - _Symptom: pages you thought were excluded leave your sitemap and gain a robots directive on the next build. Worth a look at your sitemap diff if you use the key._

- **Later parts of a split page are in the sitemap.** They were being written to disk and served while appearing in no sitemap at all. A part inherits `lastmod`, `changefreq` and `priority` structurally, because it is emitted in the same branch that decided page one was includable. Parts deliberately get no hreflang, since an alternate has to name the equivalent page and page one is not it.

  On the site this was found on, sitemap URLs went from 71 to 89.

- **`buildBacklinkIndex`'s JSDoc** described the wrong parameters and a field the function never reads. No behaviour change.

## Internal

- `applyPartLabel` gained seven tests, and mutation-testing them turned up a dead type guard that the line below it already covered. Dropped.
- Heading anchors moved to their own slugifier, `core/utils/slugify-anchor.js`, which wraps the identity one. Wikilink target slugs, page slugs and the organisation `@id` fragment are unchanged: for a key, deleting anything odd is the right behaviour, and that is what they still do.
