These feature guides build on each other. Starting from the simple site, you fill it in with assets, images and head extras, then sitemaps and drafts. The last two stand apart: debugging is what you reach for when something is off, and custom schema is a tour of a seam rather than a walkthrough.

**Every guide in this chapter starts from the site the [[simple-baseline-site | simple site tutorial]] builds.** Each page names anything it needs beyond that.
