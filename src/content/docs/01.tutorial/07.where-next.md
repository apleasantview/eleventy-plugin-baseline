## Where to go next

If you are looking to build a multilingual site, it's the [[multilingual-baseline-site | next page in this chapter]].

- Add more pages under `src/content/pages/` with front matter (`title`, `description`, `permalink`, `layout`).
- If you deploy under a subpath, set `pathPrefix` in `eleventy.config.js`.
- Once you have a production URL, update `src/_data/settings.js` so canonical URLs and the sitemap match.
- The natural next step is the [[assets-pipeline | assets pipeline guide]], which adds PostCSS and esbuild entries. After that, the [[head-and-noindex | head and noindex guide]] covers what Baseline injects into every page and how to extend it.
