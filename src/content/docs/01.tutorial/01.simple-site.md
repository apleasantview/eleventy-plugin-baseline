The shortest path from an empty folder to a working Baseline site. By the end you'll have two pages rendering through Eleventy, a tiny stylesheet bundled automatically, and a sitemap that updates itself.

This is the tutorial the feature guides build on.

---

## What you will build

- An Eleventy site with two pages: a home page, and one that gets its URL from a rule.
- Baseline wired with default options: `multilingual` off, `sitemap` on, `navigator` on in dev and off in production.
- A small stylesheet flowing through Baseline's assets pipeline, and an auto-generated `sitemap.xml` next to it.

---

## Prerequisites

- Node 22 or newer.
- npm.
- A terminal.
